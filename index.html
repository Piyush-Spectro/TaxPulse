TaxPulse: Product Management Case Study & PRD Showcase

A comprehensive product breakdown, requirements document (PRD), competitor matrix, and pitch deck for a continuous, year-round tax planning engine for Indian salaried professionals.

1. Executive Summary & Problem Statement

Most Indian taxpayers fail to optimize their taxes because planning is complex, jargon-heavy, and treated as a last-minute chore in March. Existing apps focus strictly on filing tax returns rather than offering continuous, year-round guidance.

Core Pain Points

  • Tax concepts are intimidating for young earners (22–32).
  • Planning happens only during filing season (Feb–March).
  • No feedback loop showing "what action to take next".
  • Tools prioritize compliance over learning and habit building.

TaxPulse Solution

  • 10-second Quick Tax Estimator for instant clarity.
  • Gamified 0–100 Tax Score to drive continuous engagement.
  • Personalized insights for 80C, 80D, HRA, and regime match.
  • Amendments feature to adjust for life events year-round.

2. Competitive Landscape Analysis

  • Strong filing expertise & CA connect
  • Capital gains tracking & broker sync
  • 80C planning & financial literacy
  • Advanced trader & freelancer support
  • Continuous, gamified tax optimization
    • Platform Key Strength Critical Gap / Opportunity
    ClearTax Seasonal tool only; weak in year-round engagement
    INDmoney Overwhelming UX; lacks beginner-friendly tax guidance
    ET Money Generic recommendations; no gamified tax score
    Quicko Not mainstream; high friction for simple salaried users
    TaxPulse (Us) First product offering year-round nudges + 10-sec estimator

    3. Product Requirements (Must-Have vs Nice-to-Have)

    Phase 1: Must-Have MVP Features

    1. Quick Tax Estimator: 10-second calculator for salary, age, and city.
    2. Gamified Tax Score (0-100): Dynamic score driving profile completeness.
    3. Personalized Tax Insights: Auto-generated suggestions for 80C, HRA, 80D.
    4. Amendments & Adjustments Engine: Reflect mid-year salary changes or investments.

    Phase 2: Nice-to-Have Features

    1. Form 16 / AIS Auto-Import: OCR document parsing to auto-fill details.
    2. Multi-Income Stream Tracker: Salary + Freelance + Rental + Capital Gains.
    3. AI Tax Assistant Chatbot: In-app instant query resolution.
    4. Contextual Monetization: Seamless cross-sell of ELSS & Insurance.

    Business Goals & AARRR Metric Framework

    1. Acquisition KPIs

    • Module Activation: 20% of app MAU try Tax Module within 30 days.
    • CTR: Click-through rate on tax planner banner > 15%.

    2. Engagement KPIs

    • Weekly Retention: 30% WAU for users viewing tax insights.
    • Tax Score Completion: > 40% complete setup.

    3. Data Depth KPIs

    • Profile Completeness: 40% add salary + rent + 80C details.
    • Form 16 Uploads: > 25% conversion on document import.

    4. Monetization KPIs

    • Tax Savings Products: 5–8% conversion to ELSS/NPS funds.
    • Insurance & Advisory: 2–4% conversion to health insurance/CA filing.
